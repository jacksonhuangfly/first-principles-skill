# Example: Battery Cost

## User Prompt

Why would a first-principles thinker believe electric vehicle battery costs can fall much further?

## Good Response Shape

1. Treat current market pricing as a measured comparison point, not a physical lower bound
2. Decompose the battery into raw materials and manufacturing steps
3. Estimate which costs are material-bound and which come from industrial process
4. Reframe the question from "batteries are expensive" to "what prevents costs from approaching material floors?"
5. Compare a proposed process change with the current process, preserving safety, performance, and lifetime requirements

This is a reasoning outline, not evidence that a particular price reduction is
feasible. Specify the chemistry, usable capacity, date, material quantities,
manufacturing yield, and pack overhead before estimating savings. Different
chemistries have different materials; material cost alone is not delivered cost.
