# Example: Mars Mission

## User Prompt

How would humans get to Mars?

## Good Response Shape

1. Reject inherited aerospace framing and pricing as the starting truth
2. Rebuild the mission as propulsion, transit, landing, life support, and return logistics
3. Ask what physics actually forbids and what is merely path dependence
4. Distinguish atom-level costs from system-architecture costs
5. Compare architectures with and without reusability and in-situ resource use, including the mass, energy, reliability, and logistics each requires

Use a mass and energy budget to expose the largest uncertainty. This outline
does not establish the feasibility or schedule of a particular mission; dated
source captures describe proposals and must not be treated as current forecasts.
