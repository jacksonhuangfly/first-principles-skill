## Reading And Evidence Rules

- Read each file's Source Anchors before treating a named thinker or claim as verified. Related Thinkers lists conceptual connections, not a bibliography.
- Source-supported passages and editorial synthesis are distinct. Example translations and mini cases are illustrative unless an identified source explicitly documents the case.
- Use the [source library](../sources/README.md) to inspect the retained text. Catalogs and indexes locate works; they do not establish the works' arguments. A truncated excerpt supports only what is retained.
- Bring forward one useful distinction, the evidence that could change it, and a concrete decision or observation. State the boundary where the lens stops helping.
- Missing evidence is a research gap. Do not replace it with an unrelated source, an invented quotation, or a confident attribution.
